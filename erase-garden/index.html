Erase Garden

Erase, grow flowers and discover friends in a quiet watercolor garden.

Six little discoveries

Erase Garden is a quiet watercolor game for little explorers. Six scenes are home to a fox cub, a duckling, a snail, a hedgehog, a butterfly and a unicorn. All are available from the start: choose any scene and return to your favorites.

Use broad strokes to clear each covering, sweep the pink eraser crumbs toward the seedling and help its flower bloom. Then stay and play: sway a flower, guide a butterfly to a daisy, play with the rain or draw a broad rainbow. Revealed portraits stay in your album.

For parents

There is no losing, racing against a timer or required order. The game works offline, with no ads, in-app purchases, accounts or analytics.

In the parent area, you can adjust the sound or hide portraits again after a separate confirmation. Replaying a scene normally keeps your revealed friends.

The parent area is in English. Gameplay uses pictures and gestures. The game makes no educational or therapeutic claims.
