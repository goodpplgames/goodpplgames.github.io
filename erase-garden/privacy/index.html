Erase Garden — Privacy policy

Updated: September 21, 2026. Applies to Erase Garden 0.11.2.

Developer: Good Ppl. Contact for game and privacy questions: goodpplsupport.scone011@8shield.net.

Erase Garden is an offline game with six watercolor scenes for young children. No registration or sign-in is required. This policy describes data handled by the app itself.

Data handled by the game

The game code does not collect or transmit personal data, device information or player activity to the developer or third parties. The game has no ads, analytics, crash-reporting SDKs, in-app purchases, third-party networking SDKs or internet permission.

Your sound preference and revealed portraits are saved on your device. Touch input and scene state are processed locally to run the game; no activity history is recorded. Local preferences are not sent to the developer. Android backup is disabled for app data.

Storage and deletion

Sound preferences and portrait progress remain in local app data between sessions. They do not expire automatically.

In the parent area, you can change the sound or hide portraits again after a separate confirmation. Hiding portraits does not change the sound. Replaying a scene normally preserves revealed portraits. To delete all local game data, including your sound preference, use Clear storage in the app's Android settings. The developer does not receive this local data and cannot delete it remotely from your device.

Children and third-party services

The game is intended for young children. It has no forms for names, contact details, photos, voice or other personal information, no communication with other players and no user-generated content.

Google Play and Android operate under their own policies. This policy covers the Erase Garden app, not separate data processing by the store or operating system. Links in the parent area open an external browser or email app only when an adult chooses them. Websites, hosting providers and email services operate under their own policies.

Contact

For questions about the game, local data or this policy, email developer Good Ppl: goodpplsupport.scone011@8shield.net.

If you choose to contact us by email, you decide what information to include. The game does not send emails automatically. Please avoid including children's personal information or private playtest notes unless necessary.

Policy changes

This policy may be updated if the app's behavior changes. The latest update date appears at the top.
